LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What this build stores

RimDash currently stores your personal-best score in your browser's local storage on this device. The playable browser build does not require an account and does not intentionally send gameplay scores to a server.

Audio and gameplay

Sound effects are generated locally by your browser after you interact with the game. Gameplay input is used to control the game session.

Third-party services

This website may load fonts from Google Fonts when internet access is available. Hosting providers may process standard server logs. No advertising SDK is integrated in this build.

Future releases

If analytics, advertising, accounts, or online leaderboards are added, this notice must be updated to accurately describe their data practices before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project support contact published alongside the official release. No contact email has been configured in this development package.
